About me

Hi, I'm Mikiyas.

I'm a backend and LMS engineer focused on PHP, SQL, Moodle, and Docker. I enjoy the plumbing of software: clean APIs, sane database schemas, and deployments that behave identically everywhere. Most of my recent work centers on LMS API integrations — SSO, gradebooks, enrollments, and reporting — and on backend architecture that stays maintainable as requirements grow.

I care about clear communication, honest estimates, documentation, and shipping things that are easy to hand over. If it touches a server, a database, or an LMS, I've probably built or fixed one like it.
